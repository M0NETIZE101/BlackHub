Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

At MoviesAndSeriesHub, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to provide our service:

  • Local Storage Data: We store your preferences, watchlist, and watch history locally in your browser. This data never leaves your device.
  • Usage Data: We may collect anonymous usage data to improve our service.
  • No Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, or any personally identifiable information.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain our service
  • To personalize your experience (theme preference, view settings)
  • To track your watch history and progress
  • To improve our service based on usage patterns

3. Third-Party Services

MoviesAndSeriesHub uses third-party services for video streaming and movie data:

  • TMDB API: Provides movie and TV show metadata. Their privacy policy applies.
  • Embed Providers: Autoembed.co, VidSrc, 2Embed.cc, etc. Their privacy policies apply.
  • Font Awesome: Provides icons. Their privacy policy applies.

4. Cookies

We do not use cookies. All data is stored locally in your browser's localStorage.

5. Data Storage

All data is stored locally in your browser. We do not have access to your data and do not store anything on our servers.

6. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: privacy@moviesandserieshub.com
